About Us

Baked the old way, ordered the new way

Pizza House started as a single wood-fired oven in a corner kitchen. Today we still stretch every dough ball by hand and bake in real wood-fired ovens — we've just made it easier to get one delivered to you, hot, in about 30 minutes.
